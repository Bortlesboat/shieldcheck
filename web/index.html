    What the analyzer checks

    Register full synthetic values for memo, receipt, outgoing cipher key, recipient, or order capability. Supply stdout, stderr, telemetry URL, and telemetry body text from your own test harness.

    The shared detector checks raw strings, URI representations, and canonical single-layer Base64. It cannot find unknown values, fragments, hashes, recursive encodings, or unobserved traffic.

    Up to 5 canaries and 4 captures. Each canary is limited to 4 KiB. Each capture retains at most 128 KiB; overflow makes the result incomplete and preserves findings in the retained prefix. The complete JSON input must fit within 1 MiB.
